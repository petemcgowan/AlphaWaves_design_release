### Phase 1: Google Ads Housekeeping (The Unblocker)

**1\. Reactivate the Account**

-   **Action:** Log into [ads.google.com](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fads.google.com).
    
-   **Status:** If it says "Cancelled due to inactivity," there will be a banner at the top saying "Reactivate Account." Click it.
    
-   **Billing:** You will likely need to re-verify your card details.
    
-   **Conversion Tracking (Critical):**
    
    -   Since you have Firebase in the project (from the Cairde/previous work, or just standard Google analytics), link your **Firebase** project to **Google Ads**.
        
    -   Import the event **"first\_open"** (Android) and **"first\_open"** (iOS) as Conversions.
        
    -   Why: This tells Google's AI to find people who actually open the app, not just click the ad.

### Phase 4: CapCut Editing Tips

-   **Audio Ducking:** Since you are switching soundscapes, add a **0.5s Cross-Fade** between the audio tracks in CapCut. Don't hard cut from Rain to Static.
    
-   **Captions:** Add auto-captions or manual text. 80% of people watch mobile ads with sound OFF. The text must explain what is happening ("Swipe for Noise").
    
-   **The "Finger":** Consider adding a "Touch Indicator" overlay (a semi-transparent white circle) in CapCut that follows your swipes. It helps the user understand that you are controlling the app, it's not just a slideshow.
